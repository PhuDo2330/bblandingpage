Bà started it.

In 1997 our grandmother sold bánh mì from a cart on International Boulevard. Same bread order, same pâté recipe, same stubborn opinion that broth is not ready until it is ready.

Two of her grandchildren run the kitchen now. She still comes in on Saturdays to tell us the broth needs more time.

Nấu ăn không vội được.

— You cannot rush cooking.
